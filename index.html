Installing

  1. Open CareerConsole-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". The app isn't code-signed yet. Press More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It installs and opens by itself. Sign in with your @connect.hku.hk email and follow the steps.

Good to know

  • Your career notes and CV stay on your computer. To write them, what you type is sent to a free AI model the Career Console team runs on its own computer (not a big AI company), read, and not stored.
  • That computer is shared by everyone, so each person gets a daily limit, and if it is busy or switched off the app waits and tries again by itself.
  • Job postings come from HKU NETjobs, Indeed and LinkedIn, collected for you. Nothing is searched from your computer.
  • You review everything, and you always submit the application yourself.
